Overlays animate in and out with plain CSS: `@starting-style` plus a `transition` on `overlay` and `display`, so the exit plays before the element leaves the top layer. There is no animation library. Browsers without `overlay` transitions close at once.

| Token | Value | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `--ht-duration` | 120ms | Hover and press feedback |
| `--ht-duration-in` | 240ms | A panel opening |
| `--ht-duration-out` | 140ms | A panel closing. Faster than in. |
| `--ht-ease` | `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)` | Standard easing |
| `--ht-ease-spring` | `cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.35, 0.64, 1)` | A small overshoot on the way in |

## What moves

- Panels scale up from 0.96 and grow out of the side that faces the trigger.
- The scrim behind a dialog fades in while it blurs the page by 3px.
- Footer keys in a dialog rise from the bottom edge one after another, like cassette keys.
- Menu items stagger in.
- Toasts spring in, and they can be swiped away.

## Reduced motion

`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` removes the transitions and animations. Nothing depends on them to be understood.
