Neutrals are tinted toward violet (hue 283) so surfaces and borders feel related to the accent. Colors are written in `oklch`, and every pair that holds text keeps 4.5 to 1 contrast, checked by `scripts/contrast.mjs`. Toggle the theme in the header: these swatches are live.

Mix tone colors toward `--ht-text`, never toward `black`, so dark mode keeps its contrast. Mix in `oklab` when the two colors have different hues.

## Inverse

`--ht-inverse` and `--ht-inverse-ink` are the fill and text of tooltips and neutral toasts: dark in light mode, light in dark mode.
