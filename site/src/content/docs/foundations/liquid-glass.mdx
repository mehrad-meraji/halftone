---
title: Liquid glass
description: A translucent, blurred panel look for things that float above the page.
sidebar:
  order: 5
---

Glass is for overlays only: dialog, alert dialog, sheet, popover, menu, command and select. It blurs whatever is behind, adds a bright rim and a diagonal sheen. Everything else stays solid, so there is one material per role.

Turn it on with the `glass` attribute:

```html
<ht-popover glass>
  <ht-button variant="default">Open</ht-button>
  <div slot="content">A panel with a blurred, translucent fill.</div>
</ht-popover>
```

The look comes from tokens: `--ht-glass-filter`, `--ht-glass-panel-bg`, `--ht-glass-row-bg`, `--ht-glass-rim` and `--ht-glass-shadow`. A glass dialog also gets a lighter scrim, so there is color left to blur.

Do not layer glass on glass. Two blurred panels stacked make a muddy, distracting picture.
