Every story is checked with axe in light and dark, and every text and field color pair is checked by a script. Behavior (keyboard, focus, forms, ARIA state) is checked by hand.

## How it is checked

| Check | Tool | Covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Automated rules, including text contrast | `scripts/a11y-runner.js` (axe-core, run in Storybook) | Every story, both themes |
| Contrast axe cannot see | `node scripts/contrast.mjs` | Button faces (pseudo-element fills), field borders and focus rings (non-text, 3 to 1), switch icon |
| Behavior | Manual, in Storybook | Keyboard, focus, forms, ARIA state |

Re-run both after any change to `src/tokens/tokens.css`, and keep the table in `scripts/contrast.mjs` in step with the tokens.

## Rules the components follow

- Text keeps 4.5 to 1 against its fill. Field outlines use `--ht-border-input` and reach 3 to 1 (WCAG 1.4.11).
- A control with no visible label forwards a host `aria-label` to its native element. `aria-label` is never left on a generic host.
- Tone colors are mixed toward `--ht-text`, not black, so dark mode keeps its contrast.
- The dot screen is turned off under `forced-colors` and `prefers-contrast: more`.

## Known limits

- Axe does not check keyboard operation or what a screen reader says. The behaviors were exercised by script and by hand, **not** with a screen reader.
- `ht-tooltip` adds `aria-description` to its trigger. Support is patchier outside Chromium and Safari, so never put required information in a tooltip.
- `ht-select` is a custom list. Focus moves onto the options, which is how Radix Select works too. It has not been tried with a screen reader.
- `ht-dialog` relies on the native dialog: focus is trapped and returned by the browser, but background scroll is not locked.
- `ht-tree` and `ht-radio-group` follow the WAI-ARIA patterns but have not been tried with VoiceOver or NVDA.
- Toasts that dismiss on a timer are a risk for slow readers. Use `duration: 0` for anything someone must act on. A toast can also be reached with F8 and closed with Escape.
