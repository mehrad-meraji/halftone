An empty state tells someone why there is nothing here and what to do about it. Use [`ht-empty-state`](../../components/empty-state/).

## Try it

<Stage>
  <ht-empty-state heading="No monitors yet" description="A monitor checks a URL on a schedule and tells you when it stops answering. Add one to get started.">
    <ht-button slot="actions" variant="primary">Add Monitor</ht-button>
    <ht-button slot="actions" variant="default">Learn More</ht-button>
  </ht-empty-state>
</Stage>

## Write it

- **Heading.** Say what is missing, or what to do: "No monitors yet". Not "Empty".
- **Description.** One or two sentences: why it is empty, and the next step.
- **One primary action.** At most one secondary, and it can be a link to learn more.
- **Be kind.** Never make someone feel they missed something.

## Show it

- The graphic sits on a dot screen that fades out around it. The heading, description and buttons stay on the plain surface. See the [dot screen](../../foundations/dot-screen/) rules.
- The graphic is decoration and is hidden from screen readers, so the heading must say it all.
- Inside a card, leave out the card's own header strip decoration and let the empty state sit in the body.

## Errors are not empty states

When something went wrong, use `tone="error"`. There is no dot screen, the graphic is the alert icon, and the copy is plain.

<Stage>
  <ht-empty-state tone="error" heading="Monitors could not be loaded" description="Something went wrong on our side. Try again in a minute.">
    <ht-button slot="actions" variant="default">Try Again</ht-button>
  </ht-empty-state>
</Stage>

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| "No monitors yet" with an Add Monitor button | "Nothing here" with no way forward |
| An alert icon and "Try again" for an error | A playful illustration on an error |
| One primary action | Three buttons of equal weight |
